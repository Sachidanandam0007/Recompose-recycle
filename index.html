Rules & EPR

Waste handling in India runs on rules made under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986. Here is how CPCB, State boards and EPR fit together.

CPCB

  • Runs a separate centralised EPR portal for each waste stream.
  • Grants EPR registration, where targets are declared and certificates exist.
  • Levies environmental compensation where a target is not met.
  • Audits the transaction record on the portal.

State boards (SPCB)

  • Enforce within their state, here the Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board.
  • Issue consents such as Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate.
  • Inspect facilities and check records.

Registration is separate for each regime and role. Nothing nets across streams.

A business under several regimes files on several cycles and portals, with no consolidated return.

Commonly needed

  • EPR registration on each CPCB portal that covers you
  • Consent to Establish and Consent to Operate from the State Board
  • Plastic waste processor registration, where plastic is handled
  • Hazardous waste authorisation, where it applies

General business papers

  • Company registration and CIN
  • GST registration, where applicable
  • Udyam (MSME) registration
  • Local trade or factory licence

What applies depends on your activity and materials. Check current requirements with CPCB or the State Board.

Compensation

The CPCB levies environmental compensation. Paying it does not clear the obligation: the unmet target carries forward for up to three years.

Registration at risk

Registration can be suspended or cancelled. Part of the compensation may be refunded if the shortfall is made good, and refund rules differ by stream.

General information, not legal advice. Source: GreenSutra EPR Compliance Guide (reviewed July 2026) and CPCB notifications. Confirm current rules with CPCB or the Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what we collect when you use this website or send us a request, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Details you enter in our forms: name, phone or email, area, material type and notes.
  • The email address you give us when you contact us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and arrange pickup or drop-off.
  • To keep a record of waste we receive.

Sharing and keeping

We do not sell your details. We share them only with people who need them to complete your request, or where the law requires it. We keep them only as long as needed for these purposes.

Waste Acceptance Policy

What we accept

Non-metal waste and scrap, including old newspapers and paper, rejected glass articles and used non-metallic items. We confirm each material before collection.

How to hand it over

  • Keep each stream separate: paper, glass and other items.
  • Keep paper dry. Empty and rinse glass.
  • Do not mix in food waste, medical waste, chemicals or other hazardous material.

Our right to refuse

We may refuse or return material that is mixed, unsafe or outside what we are set up to handle.

Environmental & Compliance Policy

We handle waste in line with the rules made under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, and with the requirements of the Central Pollution Control Board and the Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board that apply to our activity.

  • We keep records of the material we receive and process.
  • We keep waste streams separate to improve recycling.
  • We review the rules that apply to us when they change.
