Recompose Recycling collects and recycles non-metal waste and scrap from Coimbatore: old newspapers, rejected glass articles and used non-metallic items.

Step 1

Collect

Non-metal waste is collected from homes, offices and businesses and brought to our facility.

Step 2

Sort

Once received, the waste is separated by material: paper, glass and other non-metal items.

Step 3

Recycle

Sorted material is processed so it can go back into use instead of landfill.

Step 4

Re-use

Recycled material returns to the market as new products.

The company

Recompose Recycling Private Limited was incorporated on 1 June 2020 and is registered in Coimbatore. Active, with two directors.
